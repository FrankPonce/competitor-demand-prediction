## Planned approach

- Seed taxonomy (languages, ML frameworks, cloud, hardware/GPU, data, MLOps, research areas)
- Dictionary/regex matcher + spaCy PhraseMatcher; embedding similarity for unseen variants
- Features: `extracted_skills`, `skill_categories`, `tech_stack_tags`, `seniority_level`, `skill_frequency_vector`
- Manual spot-check of ~50 postings to estimate precision
