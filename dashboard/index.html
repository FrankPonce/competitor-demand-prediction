        06 · Methods & sources

        How this works

        Pipeline

        Snapshots of public job boards on Mondays and Thursdays → text cleaning → skill, role and seniority extraction (211-skill taxonomy) → trends, comparison, forecasts, similarity → this page. GitHub Actions rebuilds the data; Vercel serves it as static files.

        Forecasting

        Eight models (naive, drift, moving average, seasonal naive, ETS, ARIMA, harmonic regression, ensemble) compete in rolling back-tests. The winner per series is frozen with an empirical 80% interval and scored weekly. Nothing is edited after freezing.

        Compliance

        Only public, unauthenticated endpoints, robots.txt respected, polite rate limits, no personal data. Historical counts come from Epoch AI (CC BY) and the Internet Archive. Logos: Simple Icons (CC0); trademarks belong to their owners. Not affiliated with any company shown.
